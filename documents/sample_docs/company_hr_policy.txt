SECTION 1 — WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
-----------------------------------------
1.1 Standard Working Hours
    Official working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.
    The company observes a 40-hour work week.

1.2 Flexible Work Policy
    Employees may opt for flexible hours (8 AM–5 PM or 10 AM–7 PM) with
    prior approval from their department head. Core hours (11 AM–4 PM)
    are mandatory for all team meetings and collaboration.

1.3 Remote Work
    Full-time employees are eligible for up to 2 days of work-from-home
    per week after completing 6 months of service. Remote work requests
    must be submitted via the HR portal by Friday of the preceding week.

1.4 Attendance Tracking
    All employees must mark attendance using the biometric system or
    the digital HR portal. Repeated late arrivals (more than 3 per month)
    will be flagged for a performance discussion.

SECTION 2 — LEAVE POLICY
--------------------------
2.1 Annual Leave (AL)
    - Full-time employees: 18 days per calendar year
    - Interns (> 3 months): 6 days per 6-month tenure
    - Unused AL can be carried forward (maximum 9 days)
    - AL encashment allowed once per year at the time of appraisal

2.2 Sick Leave (SL)
    - 12 days per year (non-accumulative)
    - Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days
    - SL cannot be converted to cash or carried forward

2.3 Casual Leave (CL)
    - 6 days per year for personal emergencies
    - Must be applied at least 2 hours before shift start when possible
    - Cannot be combined with Privilege Leave without prior approval

2.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
    - Maternity: 26 weeks fully paid (as per Maternity Benefit Act, 1961)
    - Paternity: 10 days fully paid within 3 months of child's birth
    - Adoptive parents: 12 weeks leave for primary caregiver

2.5 Public Holidays
    TechVision observes 14 public holidays per year. A list is published
    in December for the following year. Employees working on public holidays
    are entitled to compensatory off within 30 days.

SECTION 3 — CODE OF CONDUCT
-----------------------------
3.1 Professional Behavior
    All employees must maintain a respectful, inclusive, and professional
    demeanor. Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind is strictly
    prohibited and may result in immediate termination.

3.2 Confidentiality
    Employees must not disclose proprietary information, client data, or
    internal processes to third parties. NDAs must be signed on Day 1.
    Violation of confidentiality is grounds for termination and legal action.

3.3 Conflict of Interest
    Employees must disclose any outside employment, investments, or
    relationships that may conflict with company interests. Approval must
    be obtained before accepting freelance work in the same domain.

3.4 Social Media Policy
    - Employees must not share internal project details or client names online
    - Tagging TechVision in personal posts requires marketing team approval
    - Negative or defamatory posts about the company or colleagues are prohibited

3.5 Dress Code
    Business casual attire is expected in the office. On Fridays, casual
    dress is permitted. Client-facing meetings require formal business attire.

SECTION 4 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
---------------------------------------
4.1 Salary Structure
    CTC components: Basic (40%), HRA (20%), Special Allowance (30%),
    PF Contribution (10%). Salaries are credited on the last working day.

4.2 Performance Appraisal
    Annual appraisals in April. Rating scale: 1 (Below Expectations) to
    5 (Exceptional). Increment range: 0%–25% based on rating.

4.3 Health Insurance
    Group Mediclaim policy covering employee + spouse + 2 children.
    Coverage: ₹5 Lakh per family per year. Premium fully borne by company.

4.4 Provident Fund
    Mandatory PF deduction: 12% of basic salary. Company contributes
    an equal 12%. Employees become eligible for PF withdrawal after
    5 years of continuous service.

4.5 Employee Stock Options (ESOP)
    Senior employees (Band 4 and above) are eligible for ESOPs with a
    4-year vesting schedule (25% per year, 1-year cliff).

SECTION 5 — GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
---------------------------------
5.1 Reporting
    Employees may raise grievances via the HR portal or directly email
    grievance@techvision-analytics.com. Anonymous reporting is supported.

5.2 Resolution Timeline
    - Acknowledgement within 48 hours
    - Preliminary review within 7 working days
    - Resolution or escalation within 21 working days

5.3 Anti-Retaliation
    TechVision strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who
    raises a legitimate grievance in good faith.
