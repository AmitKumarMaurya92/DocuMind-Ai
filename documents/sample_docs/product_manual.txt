1.1 About SmartTrack Pro 2000
------------------------------
The SmartTrack Pro 2000 (STP-2000-X) is a professional-grade GPS asset tracking
and fleet management device designed for commercial logistics, construction
equipment monitoring, and enterprise fleet operations.

Key Features:
- Real-time GPS tracking with 3-second update intervals
- 4G LTE Cat-M1 and NB-IoT dual connectivity
- Integrated accelerometer and gyroscope for motion detection
- Geofencing support (up to 256 zones per device)
- Engine immobilizer control via CAN Bus
- 10,000 mAh built-in battery (72-hour standby without vehicle power)
- IP67 waterproof and dustproof rating
- Operating temperature: -40°C to +85°C

2.3 Power

3.1 SIM Card Installation
--------------------------
WARNING: Always power off the device before inserting or removing the SIM card.

1. Use the SIM slot cover key to open the Nano-SIM compartment (left side panel).
2. Insert a Nano-SIM card (data plan, APN configured) into the slot gold-side down.
3. Press until you hear a click. Close and lock the cover.
4. Required APN settings must be configured via the NovaTech portal before first use.

3.2 OBD-II Installation (Plug-and-Play)
-----------------------------------------
1. Locate the OBD-II port in your vehicle (typically under the dashboard,
   driver's side, within 24 inches of the steering column).
2. Connect the included OBD-II adapter cable to the device's 26-pin connector.
3. Plug the OBD-II connector firmly into the vehicle's OBD-II port.
4. The LED indicator will blink GREEN (3 times) to confirm power.
5. Allow 60 seconds for initial GPS acquisition and server registration.

3.3 Hard-Wire Installation
---------------------------
For vehicles without OBD-II or for covert/permanent installation:
- RED wire  → Constant 12V/24V power (fused with provided 3A fuse)
- BLACK wire → Vehicle chassis ground
- ORANGE wire → Ignition switched power (for ignition detection)

Secure the device using the magnetic mount or adhesive tape (included).
Avoid mounting near metal surfaces that may block the antenna.

3.4 LED Status Indicators
---------------------------
LED Color | Pattern          | Status
Green     | Solid            | GPS fix acquired, server connected
Green     | Slow blink (2s)  | GPS searching, server connected
Blue      | Fast blink (0.5s)| Cellular data transmission
Red       | Solid            | Critical error (check logs)
Red       | Slow blink       | Low backup battery (< 20%)
Orange    | Solid            | Device in sleep/power-save mode

4.1 Geofencing
--------------
Create up to 256 geofence zones via the NovaTech Cloud Portal:
- Zone types: Circle (min radius 50m), Polygon, Corridor
- Alert triggers: Entry, Exit, or Both
- Alert channels: SMS, Email, Push Notification, Webhook

4.2 Configurable Alerts
------------------------
- Speeding: Alert when vehicle exceeds configurable speed threshold (km/h)
- Harsh Braking: Triggered when deceleration > 0.7G
- Harsh Acceleration: Triggered when acceleration > 0.5G
- Cornering: Triggered when lateral force > 0.5G
- Tampering: Alert on unauthorized device removal (accelerometer-based)
- Idle Time: Alert when engine is on but vehicle stationary for > N minutes
- Towing: Detect movement without ignition (anti-theft)

4.3 Engine Immobilizer
-----------------------
WARNING: Only qualified technicians should configure the immobilizer output.
The relay output (Digital Output 1) can be wired to interrupt the fuel pump
or ignition circuit. Immobilization can be triggered remotely via the portal
or automatically via geofence exit.
